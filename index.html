UNDERGRADUATE RESEARCHDEC 2025 — APR 2026

Transient Stability Assessment Using the Swing Equation and Machine Learning

Modelled the rotor-angle response of a 2 MW wind generator under faults using MATLAB. The study identified a simulated critical clearing time of 0.286 s. A Random Forest classifier trained on 3,456 simulated cases achieved 99.1% test accuracy in predicting stability under the modelled conditions.
